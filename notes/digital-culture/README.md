# Digital culture

Retrospective study material compiled on 2026-10-27. Historical Git author dates are editorial archive slots chosen for this collection, not evidence that the notes, experiments, forks, or software existed or were completed on those dates. Git committer dates retain the actual time of compilation. Referenced projects and APIs may postdate an archive slot. All proposed exercises remain unexecuted unless a later dated record supplies results. No participant data or research findings are claimed.

- [Make the provenance chain visible for each digital object](digital-object-provenance-chain.md) — A new documentation exercise for connecting a digital exhibit to its sources and later transformations. It proposes an auditable record rather than asserting provenance for any particular collection item.
- [Present multiple interpretations without inventing a consensus](multiple-interpretations-with-attribution.md) — A proposed exhibit writing exercise that keeps interpretations attributable and comparable. It uses existing public project themes as context and does not invent community testimony or interviews.
- [Plan a delayed memory study without confusing immersion with learning](memory-after-immersive-experience.md) — A new study outline for examining what remains after a cultural experience. All sessions, measures, and comparisons are proposed; no participants, findings, or retention effects are claimed.
- [Write context labels that expose uncertainty and scope](cultural-context-labels.md) — A new labeling exercise for separating documented context from contemporary interpretation. It proposes a field structure and review process rather than asserting facts about particular cultural objects.
- [Treat community permissions as a maintained relationship](community-permission-records.md) — A proposed documentation workflow for contributions involving community knowledge or cultural materials. It is a design exercise, not evidence of obtained permission or a substitute for legal review.
- [Let visitors choose a path through a virtual exhibition](virtual-museum-wayfinding.md) — A new navigation exercise for separating exhibition structure from movement through a simulated building. The linked museum replica is a project reference and implies no institutional affiliation.
- [Make audio meaning available in synchronized text](audio-captions-with-context.md) — A new captioning workflow exercise for cultural audio. It proposes timing, attribution, and review checks without claiming that any referenced archive currently contains verified captions.
- [Version cultural records without erasing their revision history](versioned-cultural-records.md) — A new archival design exercise for tracking source changes, editorial corrections, and revised interpretations. It specifies a proposed record structure rather than claiming a deployed preservation system.
- [Make missing archive information useful rather than invisible](archive-missing-fields.md) — A new archive quality exercise for distinguishing unknown, unrecorded, disputed, and inapplicable information. It proposes practical review queues without inventing facts about works or artists.
- [Translate exhibit labels while preserving terms and disagreements](cross-language-exhibit-labels.md) — A new editorial exercise for linked language versions of cultural labels. It proposes a reviewable translation process and does not claim completed expert translation or universal equivalence.
