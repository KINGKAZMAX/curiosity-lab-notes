# Plan a delayed memory study without confusing immersion with learning

A new study outline for examining what remains after a cultural experience. All sessions, measures, and comparisons are proposed; no participants, findings, or retention effects are claimed.

## Question

What do visitors remember after leaving an immersive cultural experience, and how does that differ from feeling engaged while inside it? This proposed study separates recall of specific content, understanding of relationships, and memory of the interface itself. It asks whether the presentation supports meaningful interpretation beyond an immediate positive reaction. The question should remain open: a vivid environment could support some memories while distracting from others, and enjoyment alone cannot settle that distinction.
